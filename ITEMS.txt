Seal Duplicate Rule
Devil's Offerings has a fixed 8% chance to offer an ELITE upgrade for a SEAL you already own. If you own no SEALS, the ELITE chance is 0. If you already own the offered SEAL, buying it upgrades that SEAL instead of taking another slot. The next copy costs twice its base price, the third costs three times, and so on. Equipped names upgrade to ELITE, then ELITE +2, ELITE +3, etc. Satanic Bible doubles the ELITE chance for the next SEAL reroll.
You can hold up to 6 SEALS. BOSS kill 2 unlocks one extra SEAL slot, and BOSS kill 4 unlocks two more, reaching the cap of 6.

Core Round Rules
Damage from TARGET difference is calculated last. SEAL damage resolves from the leftmost equipped SEAL slot to the rightmost equipped SEAL slot.
After normal penalties are calculated, whoever is furthest from the TARGET takes 10 extra damage. If several participants tie for worst guess, all tied participants take the damage.
Ipos changes this so the 3 furthest guesses are punished instead, with the furthest taking double damage.
Balam instantly eliminates non-boss DAMNED that reach both 10+ MEMORY and 10+ BOUNTY.
If all 5 opponents are eliminated in 1 round, PENTAKILL heals 5 and pays 8 SIN.
Every normal DAMNED elimination heals you for 1. Every BOSS elimination heals you for 8.
Later-BOSS ability damage increases by +1 for every 8 completed BOSS kills.
Healing cannot revive you from 0 HP. If total round damage drops you to 0, later healing effects do not keep you alive.
If you have more than 150 SIN, Devil's Offerings item prices double. For every additional 150 SIN above that, item prices double again. When your SIN falls back below a threshold, prices immediately fall back too.

Boss Scaling
The first eight special BOSS events are Zilon, Dantre, Pyros, Threon, Kalha, Serafim, Padma, and Petros/Pavlos, in random order. BOSSES arrive strictly after every 8 KOs. The SKIP button jumps past those eight special BOSS events and starts the ninth BOSS event, the Goetic/infinite phase. Normal DAMNED start at 20-60 HP, gain +5 HP to both limits after each of the first four BOSS kills, +10 HP after each of the next four BOSS kills, then +20 HP after each later BOSS kill. After the first eight BOSS kills, the resulting DAMNED HP range is also multiplied by 1.08 for every 3 later BOSS kills. BOSS HEALTH gains +20 per prior BOSS spawn through the fourth BOSS, +30 per prior BOSS spawn through the eighth BOSS, then +60 per prior BOSS spawn after that. BOSS HEALTH is doubled from the fifth BOSS onward. From BOSS 10 onward, the resulting BOSS HEALTH is also multiplied by 1.08 for every 3 BOSS numbers after BOSS 9. After the eight special BOSS events, later BOSSES are Goetic BOSSES named after one of the playable SEALS. They use the matching Goetic BOSS image, roll one fixed TARGET modifier from 0.6 to 1.2 when they spawn, keep that modifier until they die, and receive two random later-BOSS abilities. Later-BOSS ability damage increases by +1 for every 8 completed BOSS kills.

Goetic Boss Seal Lock
While a Goetic BOSS is alive, the matching player SEAL cannot trigger. The disabled SEAL appears darker in your SEAL bar until that BOSS dies.
