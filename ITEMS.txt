Seal Duplicate Rule
Devil's Offerings has a fixed 20% chance to offer an ELITE upgrade for a SEAL you already own. If you own no SEALS, the ELITE chance is 0. If you already own the offered SEAL, buying it upgrades that SEAL instead of taking another slot. The next copy costs twice its base price, the third costs three times, and so on. Equipped names upgrade to ELITE, then ELITE +2, ELITE +3, etc. Satanic Bible makes the next SEAL reroll have a 50% ELITE chance.
You can hold up to 6 SEALS. BOSS kill 2 unlocks one extra SEAL slot, and BOSS kill 4 unlocks two more, reaching the cap of 6.

Core Round Rules
Damage from TARGET difference is calculated last. SEAL damage resolves from the leftmost equipped SEAL slot to the rightmost equipped SEAL slot.
After normal penalties are calculated, whoever is furthest from the TARGET takes 10 extra damage. If several participants tie for worst guess, all tied participants take the damage.
Ipos changes this so the 3 furthest guesses are punished instead, with the furthest taking double damage.
Balam instantly eliminates non-boss DAMNED that reach both 10+ MEMORY and 10+ BOUNTY.
If all 5 opponents are eliminated in 1 round, a random owned SEAL is upgraded. If you have no SEAL yet, a random SEAL enters your SEALS instead.
Every normal DAMNED elimination heals you for 1. Every BOSS elimination heals you for 8.
For every 8 BOSS eliminations, your TARGET-difference damage is multiplied by x1.5 again. This is shown in the TARGET panel as diff dmg.
Healing cannot revive you from 0 HP. If total round damage drops you to 0, later healing effects do not keep you alive.
If you have more than 150 SIN, Devil's Offerings item prices double. For every additional 150 SIN above that, item prices double again. When your SIN falls back below a threshold, prices immediately fall back too.

Unique Boss Seals

Kalha's Seal
Kalha has Drifter personality. Each round, one random equipped SEAL is deactivated until round end, and the TARGET modifier becomes 1.2.

Padma's Seal
Padma has Stubborn personality. While Padma is alive, you cannot recover HEALTH in any way. At the start of every round, Padma heals every other DAMNED for 10% max HEALTH, and the TARGET modifier becomes 0.6.

Petros and Pavlos's Seal
Petros and Pavlos enter together as one special BOSS event. Each twin has half BOSS HEALTH and half BOSS SIN, and each borrows one other special BOSS ability. While either twin is alive, the TARGET modifier alternates every round between 0.9 and 1.1, starting at 0.9. If both twins would take lethal damage in the same round, Pavlos takes no damage for the rest of that round.

Serafim's Seal
Serafim has Caller personality. When Serafim enters play, you lose half your SIN; Serafim gains that SIN and twice that amount as HEALTH. Each round, you lose up to 2 SIN and Serafim gains the amount lost. The TARGET modifier becomes 0.7.

Dantre's Seal
Dantre has Stubborn personality. Every elimination deals 3 damage to you while Dantre is alive, and the TARGET modifier becomes 0.5.

Pyros's Seal
Pyros has Analyst personality. At the start of every round, one DAMNED receives a new random BOSS-variant SEAL while Pyros is alive, and the TARGET modifier becomes 1.0.

Threon's Seal
Threon has Drifter personality. Each round, Threon applies a hidden mystery TARGET modifier from 0.7 to 1.3. Every ARTIFACT you use has a 50% chance to malfunction, have no effect, and deal 5 damage to you.

Zilon's Seal
Zilon has Caller personality. Devil's Offerings prices cost +3 SIN for ARTIFACTS and +6 SIN for SEALS while Zilon is alive, and the TARGET modifier becomes 1.5.

Boss Scaling
The first eight special BOSS events are Zilon, Dantre, Pyros, Threon, Kalha, Serafim, Padma, and Petros/Pavlos, in random order. BOSSES arrive strictly after every 8 KOs unless Vial of Blood calls one sooner. The SKIP button jumps past those eight special BOSS events and starts the ninth BOSS event, the Goetic/infinite phase. Normal DAMNED start at 20-60 HP, gain +5 HP to both limits after each of the first four BOSS kills, +10 HP after each of the next four BOSS kills, then +20 HP after each later BOSS kill. BOSS HEALTH gains +20 per prior BOSS spawn through the fourth BOSS, +30 per prior BOSS spawn through the eighth BOSS, then +60 per prior BOSS spawn after that. BOSS HEALTH is doubled from the fifth BOSS onward. After the eight special BOSS events, later BOSSES are Goetic BOSSES named after one of the playable SEALS. They use the matching Goetic BOSS image, roll one fixed TARGET modifier from 0.6 to 1.2 when they spawn, keep that modifier until they die, and deal 1 damage to you at end of round just by existing.

Goetic Boss Seal Lock
While a Goetic BOSS is alive, the matching player SEAL cannot trigger. The disabled SEAL appears darker in your SEAL bar until that BOSS dies.
