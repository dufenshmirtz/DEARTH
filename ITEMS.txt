Seal Duplicate Rule
Devil's Offerings has a fixed 20% chance to offer an ELITE upgrade for a Seal you already own. If you own no Seals, the ELITE chance is 0. If you already own the offered Seal, buying it upgrades that Seal instead of taking another slot. The next copy costs twice its base price, the third costs three times, and so on. Equipped names upgrade to ELITE, then ELITE +2, ELITE +3, etc. The Hand of Glory makes the next Seal reroll have a 50% ELITE chance.
You can hold up to 6 Seals. Boss kill 2 unlocks one extra Seal slot, and boss kill 4 unlocks two more, reaching the cap of 6.

Seal Healing ELITE Rule
For every Seal with healing, the base heal stays fixed when upgraded. Each ELITE level gives SIN equal to the base heal divided by 5, rounded up, whenever that Seal heal triggers.

Core Round Rules
Damage from target difference is calculated last. Seal damage resolves from the leftmost equipped Seal slot to the rightmost equipped Seal slot.
After normal penalties are calculated, whoever is furthest from the target takes 10 extra damage. If several participants tie for worst guess, all tied participants take the damage.
If all 5 opponents are eliminated in 1 round, a random owned Seal is upgraded. If you have no Seal yet, a random Seal enters your Seals instead.
Healing cannot revive you from 0 HP. If total round damage drops you to 0, later healing effects do not keep you alive.

Unique Boss Seals

Dantre's Seal
Dantre has Stubborn personality. Every elimination deals 3 damage to you while Dantre is alive, and the target modifier becomes 0.5.

Pyros's Seal
Pyros has Analyst personality. At the start of every round, one SINNER receives a new random boss-variant Seal while Pyros is alive, and the target modifier becomes 1.0.

Threon's Seal
Threon has Drifter personality. Each round, Threon applies a hidden mystery target modifier from 0.7 to 1.3. Every Artifact you use has a 50% chance to malfunction, have no effect, and deal 5 damage to you.

Zilon's Seal
Zilon has Caller personality. Devil's Offerings prices cost +3 SIN for Artifacts and +6 SIN for Seals while Zilon is alive, and the target modifier becomes 1.5.

Boss Scaling
The first four bosses are Zilon, Dantre, Pyros, and Threon, and bosses arrive strictly after every 8 KOs unless Witch Bottle calls one sooner. After the fourth boss, all later bosses are Goetic bosses named after one of the playable Seals. They use the matching Goetic boss image, roll one fixed target modifier from 0.6 to 1.2 when they spawn, keep that modifier until they die, and have double boss health.

Goetic Boss Seal Lock
While a Goetic boss is alive, the matching player Seal cannot trigger. The disabled Seal appears darker in your Seal bar until that boss dies.
