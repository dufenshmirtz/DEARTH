# DEARTH glossary: the game's terms in every language

Decided Nov 2026 (Greek picked by the owner on the glossary page; German, Spanish and Chinese follow the same logic).
The words live in `locales/<code>.js` (top block); other forms that should be styled as keywords are in `LOCALE_KEYWORDS`.
Change a term here and in every locale file at once, and check Satan's Pact lexicon (`SATAN_PACT_WORDS` in `09-satan-pact.js`).

## The logic
- Stay as close to the English as the language allows. Words the English uses as loanwords for gamers stay English:
  BOSS, CRITICAL, KO (in every language, also Chinese). Reroll stays English only in Greek; German, Spanish and Chinese
  have a word gamers know (owner's call, Nov 24).
- Keywords are written in capitals in the translated texts, as in English (Greek capitals without accents), so they get the keyword style.
- BOUNTY is the burden a DAMNED carries (Greek Φορτίο), not a reward: LADUNG / CARGA / 负担.
- DAMNED are the condemned (Greek ΚΑΤΑΔΙΚΑΣΜΕΝΟΙ): VERURTEILTE / CONDENADOS / 罪人.
- MASSACRE (was PENTAKILL): all five DAMNED fall in one round.
- SEAL names: "Seal of Bael" becomes Σφραγίδα του Βάαλ / Siegel des Bael / Sello de Bael / 巴力之印. Demon names: Greek and Chinese use
  their own spelling, German and Spanish keep the Latin one.
- Chinese: the keyword SEAL is 印记 and POISON is 毒素 (two characters), because Chinese keywords are found anywhere in a text and
  a single 印 or 毒 would light up inside SEAL names and other words.
