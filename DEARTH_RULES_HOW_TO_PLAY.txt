DEARTH
Rules and How to Play


1. What Dearth Is

Dearth is a number-guessing survival game set around a cursed table in purgatory.
You are trying to survive God's endless guessing game while the Devil offers help
through SEALS and ARTIFACTS. Every enemy at the table is one of the DAMNED.
Eliminating DAMNED sends them away and earns SIN, which is used to buy more power.

There are two main modes:

Arcade
The main roguelike mode. You play alone against five DAMNED controlled by the game.
The run continues until you die. Bosses appear throughout the run, then the game
eventually reaches the Jesus and Satan final phase after the Goetic boss sequence.

PvP
A party mode hosted on the PC. Other players join from phones, take the DAMNED slots,
and play a stripped-down last-player-standing version of the guessing game.


2. Core Guessing Rule

Every living participant secretly chooses an integer guess.

In Arcade, the player normally guesses from 0 to 100.
In PvP, every living player guesses from 0 to 100.

After guesses are submitted, the TARGET is calculated:

TARGET = rounded up average of counted guesses x current modifier + target changes

The default Arcade modifier is 0.8, but BOSSES, ARTIFACTS, and some rules can change it.
The PvP modifier is one of 0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 1.0, 1.1, or 1.2.

All important numbers are rounded up to whole integers.

After the TARGET is known, each participant eventually takes TARGET-difference damage:

Damage = absolute difference between that participant's final guess and the TARGET

Example:
If the TARGET is 47 and your final guess is 40, your TARGET-difference damage is 7.

In Arcade, TARGET-difference damage is always calculated last, after SEALS,
ARTIFACTS, CRITICAL damage, worst guess damage, and other special effects.


3. Arcade Mode Goal

Stay alive as long as possible.

You start with:

100 HEALTH
8 SIN
3 SEAL slots
5 ARTIFACT inventory slots
2 ARTIFACT uses per round

The board starts with five non-boss DAMNED.
If your HEALTH reaches 0, you lose. Healing cannot save you after you have already
reached 0 from the total damage of a round. On Game Over, you can restart or inspect
the final board state.


4. Arcade Round Flow

Each round follows this order:

1. Start of round effects happen.
   Some SEALS, BOSS abilities, reveals, poison, MEMORY effects, and marked effects
   happen at the start of the round.

2. You see any revealed guesses.
   You always know one random non-boss DAMNED guess. Some SEALS reveal more.
   Normally BOSSES cannot have their guesses revealed unless a SEAL says otherwise.

3. You choose your guess.
   Enter a number and press Guess. Invalid guesses show a popup.

4. Guesses and TARGET are revealed.
   The DAMNED guesses, your guess, the average, the modifier, and the TARGET are locked
   in for the round. Damage is not applied yet.

5. ARTIFACT phase.
   You may use ARTIFACTS before penalties are applied. Some ARTIFACTS need you to pick
   a DAMNED. You can cancel a target pick if you change your mind. When finished,
   press Ready.

6. Damage and rewards resolve.
   CRITICAL hits, worst guess penalties, SEALS, ARTIFACT effects, and finally
   TARGET-difference damage are applied. Dead DAMNED stay visible until Next Round.

7. Next Round.
   Press Next Round to replace eliminated DAMNED, reroll Devil's Offerings, update
   bot MEMORY, and begin the next round.


5. Arcade CRITICAL Rule

If a participant's guess exactly matches the right integer TARGET, that participant
hits CRITICAL.

In Arcade, CRITICAL normally deals 10 damage to everyone else.

SEALS can change this. For example:

Seal of Orias expands your CRITICAL window.
Seal of Glasya-Labolas makes your CRITICAL deal extra damage.
Seal of Eligos can mirror DAMNED CRITICAL hits for you and protect you from DAMNED
CRITICAL damage.

When a DAMNED hits CRITICAL, the guess label and guess number turn red.


6. Worst Guess Penalty

After normal calculations, whoever is furthest from the TARGET takes 10 extra damage.
If several participants are tied for worst guess, all of them take the penalty.

Seal of Ipos changes this rule so the three furthest guesses are punished instead.
With Ipos, the furthest takes 20 damage and the next two take 10 damage. Its ELITE
levels double those damage values.

Seal of Andras makes you immune to worst guess penalty damage when it triggers.


7. SEALS

SEALS are passive items.

They stay equipped and trigger automatically when their condition is met.
You can sell a SEAL for part of its value.
SEALS resolve from the leftmost equipped SEAL slot to the rightmost equipped SEAL slot.

You begin with 3 SEAL slots.
You can eventually reach 6 SEAL slots.
The current rule gives you +1 SEAL slot every 2 BOSS kills, up to the cap of 6.

If you buy a SEAL that you already own, it does not take a new slot. It upgrades the
existing SEAL into ELITE instead.

Devil's Offerings has a fixed 20% chance to offer an ELITE upgrade for a SEAL you own.
If you own no SEALS, the ELITE chance is 0.

Satanic Bible changes the next SEAL reroll to a 50% ELITE chance.

Most flat damage SEALS scale strongly with ELITE levels. In the current rules, flat
damage effects generally double each ELITE level unless the SEAL description says
otherwise.

Some SEALS track their lifetime contribution. Hover a SEAL slot to see its name,
description, sell price, and any tracked total such as damage dealt, SIN gained, or
healing done.


8. ARTIFACTS

ARTIFACTS are active items.

They are consumed when used.
You can hold up to 5 ARTIFACTS by default.
You can use 2 ARTIFACTS per round by default.
Some SEALS can change both limits.

ARTIFACT values scale as the run progresses:

Every 4 BOSS kills, numeric ARTIFACT effects increase by 20%, rounded down.

Example:
A 10 damage ARTIFACT becomes 12 after 4 BOSS kills.

Some ARTIFACTS target a DAMNED. If you do not want to finish choosing a target,
you can cancel the ARTIFACT selection.


9. Devil's Offerings

Devil's Offerings is the shop.

It normally contains:

1 SEAL
2 ARTIFACTS

Some SEALS can add more shop slots.

You can reroll the shop for SIN.
Reroll cost starts at 1 SIN each round and increases by 1 after each manual reroll
that round. The cost resets when the next round begins.

The shop automatically rerolls every round.

If you have more than 150 SIN, shop prices double.
For every additional 150 SIN, prices double again.
If your SIN falls back below a threshold, prices fall back too.

While Satan is alive in the final phase, Devil's Offerings is disabled.


10. SIN, BOUNTY, and Rewards

SIN is your money.

DAMNED have BOUNTY values shown on their cards. When a DAMNED dies, you usually gain
SIN based on its displayed BOUNTY and any active payout multipliers.

BOSSES show split bounty, such as:

20+5 SIN

The first number is the BOSS bounty.
The second number is the regular bounty side. BOUNTY-related SEALS use the regular
bounty side unless their description says otherwise.

Killing a normal DAMNED heals you for 1.
Killing a BOSS heals you for 8.

If all five enemies are eliminated in the same round, PENTAKILL triggers.
PENTAKILL shows a popup, heals you for 8, and gives you 8 SIN.

The game shows popups for damage, healing, SIN changes, BOUNTY changes, and special
sources. Hovering these popups shows a source breakdown when available.


11. MEMORY

DAMNED remember previous rounds.

At Next Round, each living DAMNED gains MEMORY of the round that just ended.
Normal non-boss DAMNED have limited MEMORY.
BOSSES remember the whole game up to that point.

MEMORY includes things like:

The DAMNED's own guess
Your guess
The TARGET
The modifier
The table average
The DAMNED average

DAMNED use MEMORY to guess more intelligently. They also pay attention to the current
modifier, so they do not blindly copy a previous TARGET that was created under a
different modifier.

Some SEALS create, punish, reward, or convert MEMORY.


12. DAMNED Personalities

Each normal DAMNED has a personality that influences its guesses.

Analyst
Uses previous TARGET movement and table behavior. Analysts are the most pattern-based.

Follower
Pays attention to your previous guesses and drifts toward the recent TARGET.

Stubborn
Has a preferred anchor number and resists moving too far away from it.

Drifter
Uses the recent TARGET and table average but swings more than stable personalities.

Caller
Watches the DAMNED average and tries to pull with the group.

Anchor
Uses a more fixed internal number and adapts more slowly.

BOSSES use personalities too, but they play somewhat smarter than normal DAMNED.


13. Boss Progression

BOSSES arrive strictly after every 8 KOs, unless Vial of Blood causes an early BOSS.

The first eight special BOSS events happen in random order:

Zilon
Dantre
Pyros
Threon
Kalha
Serafim
Padma
Petros and Pavlos

After those eight, the game enters the Goetic BOSS phase.
Goetic BOSSES are named after SEALS. When a Goetic BOSS is alive, the matching player
SEAL is disabled and appears darker in your SEAL bar.

After the Goetic BOSS sequence, the final Jesus and Satan phase begins.
Only Jesus and Satan are on the board.
They have infinite HEALTH, so damage dealt to them is tracked instead of killing them.
The final phase modifier is 0.666.
Jesus's guess counts three times for the TARGET average.
Satan disables Devil's Offerings.
Satan has infinite SIN and Jesus has negative infinite SIN.

The SKIP button in the round panel is a testing/progression button:

First press jumps past the eight special BOSSES into the first Goetic BOSS.
Second press jumps to the Jesus and Satan phase.


14. Scaling

Normal DAMNED begin with HP in the 20 to 60 range.

After each of the first 4 BOSS kills, normal DAMNED HP limits increase by 5.
After each of the next 4 BOSS kills, normal DAMNED HP limits increase by 10.
After the first 8 BOSS kills, normal DAMNED HP limits increase by 20 per later
BOSS kill.

BOSS HEALTH also scales as the run progresses.
Each prior BOSS spawn adds +20 BOSS HEALTH through the fourth BOSS, +30 through
the eighth BOSS, and +60 after the first 8 BOSSES. BOSS HEALTH is doubled from
the fifth BOSS onward.

After the first 8 BOSSES, later BOSSES deal 1 damage to you at the end of each
round just by existing.

Every 8 BOSS kills, your TARGET-difference damage multiplier increases by x1.5.
The current TARGET-difference damage percentage appears in the TARGET panel as
diff dmg.


15. Special Bosses

Zilon
Devil's Offerings prices cost +3 SIN for ARTIFACTS and +6 SIN for SEALS.
The TARGET modifier becomes 1.5.
Personality: Caller.

Dantre
Every elimination deals 3 damage to you.
The TARGET modifier becomes 0.5.
Personality: Stubborn.

Pyros
At the start of each round, one DAMNED receives a random BOSS-variant SEAL.
The TARGET modifier becomes 1.0.
Personality: Analyst.

Threon
Each round, Threon applies a mystery TARGET modifier from 0.7 to 1.3.
Every ARTIFACT you use has a 50% chance to malfunction, do nothing, and deal
5 damage to you.
Personality: Drifter.

Kalha
Each round, one random equipped SEAL is deactivated until the round ends.
The TARGET modifier becomes 1.2.
Personality: Drifter.

Serafim
When Serafim enters, you lose half your SIN. Serafim gains that SIN and gains twice
that amount as HEALTH. Each round after that, you lose up to 2 SIN and Serafim gains
the amount lost.
The TARGET modifier becomes 0.7.
Personality: Caller.

Padma
You cannot recover HEALTH while Padma is alive. Padma heals every other DAMNED for
10% max HEALTH at the start of each round.
The TARGET modifier becomes 0.6.
Personality: Stubborn.

Petros and Pavlos
They enter together as one BOSS event. Each has half BOSS HEALTH and half BOSS SIN.
Each twin borrows another special BOSS ability.
While either twin is alive, the modifier alternates each round between 0.9 and 1.1,
starting at 0.9.
If both twins would take lethal damage in the same round, Pavlos takes no damage for
the rest of that round.


16. PvP Mode

PvP is hosted on the PC.
Players join from phones, enter names, and take slots.
Before the game starts, the host can add or remove slots.
When the host starts, empty slots become bot-controlled DAMNED.

PvP has no SEALS, no shop, no MEMORY economy, and no BOUNTY economy.
Everyone starts with 100 HEALTH.
Dead players are eliminated and do not participate anymore.
Last player standing wins.

If everyone dies in the same round, the winner is the player who had the least HEALTH
at the start of that round.


17. PvP Round Flow

1. Guess phase.
   Every living player submits a secret guess. Submitted guesses are not shown yet.
   A player is only marked Ready.

2. Automatic reveal.
   When everyone is ready, the host automatically reveals the first TARGET unless
   Wait is turned on.

3. ARTIFACT choice.
   Every living player gets two random ARTIFACTS plus Skip. They choose one.
   Choices stay hidden until everyone is ready.

4. Automatic resolution.
   When everyone is ready, ARTIFACTS resolve automatically unless Wait is turned on.

5. Summary.
   The host can press Next Round, or the game starts the next round automatically
   after 10 seconds unless Wait is on.


18. PvP Modifier Rule

The PvP modifier changes on round 1 and then every 3 rounds.
If a TARGET reaches or passes 100, the modifier changes again next round and the
round counter for the next modifier change resets.

Possible modifiers:

0.7
0.8
0.9
1.0
1.1
1.2

After a modifier of 1.0, 1.1, or 1.2, the next modifier must be below 1.0.
After a modifier below 1.0, the next modifier must be randomly higher than the current
one.
The same modifier should not be picked twice in a row.


19. PvP CRITICAL Rule

PvP has two CRITICAL checks.

First CRITICAL
After the first TARGET is revealed, anyone who guessed the exact first TARGET hits
CRITICAL and deals 5 damage to every enemy.

Final CRITICAL
After ARTIFACTS alter the round and the final TARGET is calculated, anyone who guessed
the exact final TARGET hits FINAL CRITICAL and deals 5 damage to every enemy.

Then normal TARGET-difference damage is applied.


20. PvP ARTIFACTS

Each PvP ARTIFACT has a 50% chance to fail.
If a player skipped ARTIFACT use last round, their ARTIFACT this round casts for sure.

Current PvP ARTIFACTS:

Demon Bowl
If it casts, deal 3 damage to everyone else.

Sigillum Dei Aemeth
If it casts, change the final TARGET by +5.

John Dee's Obsidian Mirror
If it casts, change the final TARGET by -5.

Witch in a Bottle
If it casts, you take 50% less damage this round.

The Black-Hilted Knife
If it casts, deal 5 damage to every player tied closest to the first TARGET.

Null Vote
If it casts, pick a player whose guess does not count for the final TARGET.
The log says who Null Vote was cast on.

Skip
Use no ARTIFACT this round. Your next round ARTIFACT casts for sure.


21. Interface Tips

Hover SEALS, ARTIFACTS, BOSS passives, damage bubbles, healing bubbles, MEMORY bubbles,
and SIN bubbles to see detailed descriptions and sources.

The TARGET panel shows the current TARGET, previous TARGET, modifier, calculation,
and TARGET-difference damage multiplier.

The pause menu can resume, restart, switch modes, return to the main menu, and change
sound settings.

The sound settings are saved during the session.

On mobile Arcade, hold the phone horizontally. The mobile layout focuses on the board,
SEALS, top indicators, and a button for Devil's Offerings.


22. Quick Beginner Strategy

Watch the revealed guess and the previous TARGET.

If the modifier is low, the TARGET tends to sit below the raw average.
If the modifier is high, the TARGET can jump above what the table seems to be saying.

Use ARTIFACTS before pressing Ready if they can move the TARGET closer to your guess
or push DAMNED away from it.

Do not hoard too much SIN forever. Above 150 SIN, shop prices start doubling.

SEALS that trigger often are usually better early.
SEALS with damage scaling, MEMORY scaling, or BOUNTY scaling become stronger later.

BOSSES change the rules hard. Hover them as soon as they appear.

If the table looks lethal, remember that hitting 0 HEALTH cannot be undone by healing
later in the same round.
